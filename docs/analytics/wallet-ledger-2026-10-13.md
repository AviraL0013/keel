# Public-wallet ledger gate, 2026-10-13

`EYELER_ANALYTICS_WALLET_LEDGER_ENABLED` defaults to `false`. The migration is additive. While the flag is off, the current rollup worker uses its original event selector and writes no wallet-ledger rows; the public wallet routes keep their existing behavior.

When enabled, the worker reuses its bounded HyperSync rollup reads to store compact position-event rows in the existing Postgres database. Old aggregate-only ranges are scanned newest first. A range becomes wallet-indexed only after an atomic write against the same canonical end-block hash. Pruning the 35-day coverage row cascades to its wallet events. The public trade route pages indexed candidates and proves each displayed account owner at the event block. Gross settlement is price PnL plus funding, before fees, for creation-linked accounts only. It is returned only when the complete 30-day window, owner scan, every settlement type, and every exact-block owner proof pass. Win rate, net PnL, and drawdown remain unavailable without full episode and fee reconciliation.

**Activation gate:** The [read-only HyperSync sample](wallet-history-feasibility-2026-10-13.md) projects about 11.6 million selected logs over 30 days. Position events alone could produce several million ledger rows. Exact Postgres bytes, additional Railway cost per day, and remaining trial credit are unmeasured; Railway CLI could not reach its control plane during this implementation. Keep the flag off until a representative range is written to a bounded staging database, storage growth and query latency are measured, and the expected 30-day usage fits the remaining budget. Enabling it on the existing production database without that check could exhaust the trial and disrupt the already working protocol dashboard.

The reader deliberately caps a page at five exact-block ownership proofs in the HTTP route and a gross-settlement attempt at 20 events. Large wallets may still show a formal unavailable reason. These limits prevent a public request from launching another whole-window scan or an unbounded archival RPC burst. They do not satisfy the bounty's full arbitrary-wallet historical-performance requirement.
